# Floristry

Inspired by SirSengir's classic mod, [Forestry](https://github.com/ForestryMC/ForestryMC), this is also a mod about bees and breeding. However, in Floristry you won't be breeding the bees themselves. You'll be using Minecraft bees to breed magical flowers with a wide variety of mysterious effects. And that's to say nothing about the honey your bees can make from them!

**This mod is in a very early stage!**

Currently, this mod just adds a chance for vanilla Minecraft flowers to spread (duplicate) when bees pollinate them. As I continue working on it, I'll be adding 4 tiers of *special flowers* that can only be obtained by cross-breeding other flowers together. When all the special flowers are in the game, I'll start working on mechanics for *special honey* which can be made by bees that gather nectar from special flowers.
